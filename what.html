What are E-Cigarettes?



If you haven't heard or've seen E-Cigarettes or vapes before, they are battery-operated alternatives to regular cigarettes, which were initially intended to be used to help those trying to quit nicotine. Unfortunately, E-cigarettes have only introduced a wider range of (especially young) individuals to nicotine addiction, as a majority of "flavours" can be found with nicotine in them.

How do they work?

E-Cigarettes work by lighting a liquid inside a housing, creating a vapour which is then inhaled by the user. Some E-cigarettes themselves are devices which can be bought and used with seperate "juice", others are simply sold with juice already inside them, and when depleted are disposed of.

It's important to know that the former, more common and abundant type of E-Cigarette (due to its low-cost), commonly referred to as "disposable vapes", have been creating piles of E-waste and trash.

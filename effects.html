Health effects

Negative health effects of vaping E-Cigarettes include Lung damage, Nicotine addiction and then withdrawl, negative effects on brain development, negative impacts on mental health, and so-on. There are no health benefits to E-Cigarettes

Vaping withdrawl symptoms

  • Lightheaded
  • Extreme urge to vape
  • Headaches
  • Fatigue
  • Shakiness
